Knotty

Know where you're knitting

A companion app for working knitting and filet crochet motifs from charts, without ever losing your place.

Knotty turns a photo of any chart — colorwork, lace, filet crochet, cables — into a position-aware grid you can knit from, one stitch window at a time.

What Knotty does

If you've ever knitted or crocheted from a charted pattern, you know the problem: the chart is dense, symmetrical, and repetitive, and one glance away is enough to lose your row. The paper-and-ruler trick only gets you so far.

Knotty replaces the ruler. Upload your chart, tell the app a few things about how the piece is constructed, and knit from a moving window that always shows exactly the next stitches — cropped straight from your own chart, enlarged and unambiguous. Because Knotty never tries to interpret what's in a cell, it works identically for stranded colorwork, lace symbols, filet crochet diagrams, or plain textured charts.

Calibrate once, trust it forever

The heart of Knotty is calibration — a new concept for chart apps. Instead of redrawing your chart or guessing at it with image recognition, you align a grid over your actual photo: drag the bounds around the chart, set the cell size, and verify against a numbered overlay so an off-by-one can't sneak past you. You see immediately whether the grid lines up with the real chart, before you've knitted a single stitch from it.

Mark inactive stitches

Real charts aren't rectangles. Crown decreases, gusset triangles, jagged lace edges — Knotty lets you paint cells as inactive so the app knows they're not part of the knitted shape. The stitch window simply skips them, and your row counts stay honest even through irregular shapes.

Knit with a moving window

In knit mode, a highlighted window shows your current stitches, with their actual content cropped from the source image and scaled up to a readable size. Step forward, step back, or jump to the next row — the app flips reading direction automatically for flat knitting and keeps your place saved continuously, so closing the app mid-row is safe.

Mark cells and jump anywhere

Flag individual stitches you want to come back to, and use the direct position editor to jump your cursor to any row and stitch — correct a mistake, resume from an arbitrary point, or hop freely between the regions of a multi-part project (a mitten's gusset and main chart, say) with each region's progress tracked independently.

Support & contact

knottyapp@gmail.com

We aim to respond to support requests within a few business days.

How do I restore a purchase?

If you've previously purchased Knotty's premium features and they aren't showing up (for example, after reinstalling or switching devices), open Settings in the app, tap Unlock, and then tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same App Store / Google Play account you originally purchased with. If the purchase still doesn't appear, email us at knottyapp@gmail.com with your store receipt and we'll sort it out.

How do I delete my data?

Your projects, chart photos, calibration data, and progress are stored locally on your device — Knotty has no account system and no cloud backup. Deleting the app from your device deletes all of that data.

The only data that leaves your device is: what's needed to process and restore in-app purchases, handled by our payment provider; optional crash reports and usage analytics, which are off by default and send nothing unless you turn them on in Settings → General; and any message you choose to send from Settings → Send feedback. None of it includes your projects, chart photos, or notes. See the Privacy Policy for the detail on each.

To request deletion of any data that has been transmitted, email knottyapp@gmail.com and we will process your request within the timeframes required by law.

How do I report a bug or suggest something?

Open Settings → Send feedback in the app and write us a message. It's sent anonymously and includes your app version and device model so we can reproduce the problem — no name, no email, nothing you've made.

Because it's anonymous, we can't write back. If you'd like an answer, email knottyapp@gmail.com instead (there's a link on the same screen).
